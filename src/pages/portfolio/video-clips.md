Visual fragments created on the precipice of graduation.

 I was in an nostalgic and slight emo phase of my life (maybe I still am). Heavily influenced by the music videos I love, my aim was to capture a hazy fever dream rather than a structured video essay.

I miss my 2024. Hopefully you don't.
